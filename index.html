How a Thai syllable is read

Every consonant belongs to a class. The class, the vowel length, the final sound and any tone mark together decide the tone. Tap a word in your cards to see this worked out for each syllable.

Mid class (9 letters) High class (11) Low class (24) The number on a letter shows how often it appears in your lyrics.

Live syllables end in a long vowel or in m, n, ng, i, o. Dead syllables end in a short vowel or in k, t, p. A silent ห or อ in front of a low consonant (หน, หม, อย) changes its class.

Vowels

Pronunciation letters: ʉ like "ue" with unrounded lips · ɛ like "a" in "cat" · ɔ like "aw" in "law" · ə like "er" in "her" · bp and dt are unaspirated p and t (no puff of air). Choose "Simple" above the cards to spell these as ue, ae, aw, oe.
